Shloka HR Suite Notification Automation

Objective
This document defines the dynamic notification system for the Shloka HR Suite to automate 
employee, manager, and HR reminders based on attendance, shifts, breaks, birthdays, 
anniversaries, and leave workflows.

Global Notification Standards

Supported Channels
• Push Notification (Mobile App)
• Email (for leave workflows)

Dynamic Variables / Placeholders
Use the following placeholders dynamically from backend data:
• {employee_name}
• {manager_name}
• {shift_start_time}
• {shift_end_time}
• {break_start_time}
• {break_end_time}
• {team_member_name}
• {occasion_type} → Birthday / Work Anniversary
• {leave_applicant_name}
• {leave_type}
• {leave_dates}
• {company_name}

Core Logic Rules
• Prevent duplicate notifications for the same trigger event.
• Use employee timezone/location-based shift timing.
• If shift timing is updated, notifications should auto-adjust.
• Notifications must only trigger for active employees.
• Exclude employees on approved leave from attendance reminders.
• Track notification status: Pending, Sent, Read, Dismissed.

Notification Automation Matrix

1. 15 Minutes Before Shift Starts

Trigger Condition:
Current time = Shift Start Time - 15 minutes

Audience:
Employee, Manager, HR

Title:
Your shift starts in 15 minutes 

Body:
Hi {employee_name}, your shift begins at {shift_start_time}. Please be ready to clock in on 
time.

Automation Rule:
Run once daily per scheduled shift.

2. 30 Minutes After Shift Starts (If Employee Not Logged In)

Trigger Condition:
Current time = Shift Start Time + 30 minutes AND no check-in recorded

Audience:
Employee, Manager, HR

Title:
You haven’t clocked in yet 

Body:
Hi {employee_name}, you missed your scheduled login at {shift_start_time}. Please clock in 
immediately or contact your manager.

Automation Rule:
Only if attendance status = Absent / Not Logged In

3. 15 Minutes Before Shift Ends

Trigger Condition:
Current time = Shift End Time - 15 minutes

Audience:
Employee, Manager, HR

Title:
Your shift ends in 15 minutes 

Body:
Hi {employee_name}, your shift ends at {shift_end_time}. Please complete pending tasks and 
prepare to clock out.

Automation Rule:
Only if check-in exists AND check-out missing

4. 30 Minutes After Shift Ends (If Employee Not Logged Out)

Trigger Condition:
Current time = Shift End Time + 30 minutes AND no check-out recorded

Audience:
Employee, Manager, HR

Title:
Shift ended 30 minutes ago 

Body:
Hi {employee_name}, your scheduled shift ended at {shift_end_time}. If you’re continuing with 
overtime, no action is needed right now. If you’ve completed work for the day, please remember 
to clock out to keep your attendance updated.

Automation Rule:
Only if check-in exists AND check-out missing

5. 5 Minutes Before Break Starts

Trigger Condition:
Current time = Break Start Time - 5 minutes

Audience:
Employee, Manager, HR

Title:
Break starts in 5 minutes 

Body:
Hi {employee_name}, your scheduled break begins at {break_start_time}. Please wrap up 
current tasks.

Automation Rule:
Only if break_start_time doesn’t exists.

6. 5 Minutes Before Break Ends

Trigger Condition:
Current time = Break End Time - 5 minutes

Audience:
Employee, Manager, HR

Title:
Break ends in 5 minutes 

Body:
Hi {employee_name}, your break ends at {break_end_time}. Please be ready to resume work.

Automation Rule:
Only if break_start_time exists AND break_end_time is missing

7. Break Exceeded by 20 Minutes

Trigger Condition:
Current time = Total Break Duration + 20 minutes 

Audience:
Employee, Manager, HR

Title:
Your break time has been exceeded by 20 minutes 

Body:
Hi {employee_name}, your scheduled break time has now been exceeded by 20 minutes. Please 
resume work immediately.

8. Birthday & Work Anniversary Notifications

Trigger Condition:
Employee birthday date OR joining date matches current date

Audience:
All Employees (excluding optionally self)

Title Options:
• Celebrate {team_member_name}’s Birthday today 
• Celebrate {team_member_name}’s Work Anniversary today 

Body Options:
• Wish {team_member_name} a very happy birthday and make their day special!
• Join us in celebrating {team_member_name}’s work anniversary today.

Self-Notification:

Title:
Happy {occasion_type}, {employee_name}! 

Body:
Wishing you a fantastic {occasion_type} from all of us at {company_name}.

9. Holiday Notifications

Audience:
Employee, Manager, HR

Title Options:
Happy {holiday_title}, {employee_name}! 

Body:
Wishing you a fantastic {holiday_title} from {company_name}.

10. Leave Request Notification for Managers & HR

Trigger Condition:
Employee submits leave request

Audience:
Reporting Manager + HR

Title:
New leave request submitted 

Body:
{leave_applicant_name} has requested {leave_type} leave for {leave_dates}. Please review and 
take action.

Action Buttons:
• Approve
• Reject
• View Details

11. Leave Approval / Rejection Notification for Employee

Trigger Condition:
Manager or HR updates leave status

Audience:
Employee

Approval Title:
Your leave request was approved 

Approval Body:
Hi {employee_name}, your {leave_type} leave for {leave_dates} has been approved.

Rejection Title:
Your leave request was declined 

Rejection Body:
Hi {employee_name}, your {leave_type} leave for {leave_dates} was not approved. Please 
contact your manager or HR.

Note

Implement this using:
• Node Cron / BullMQ / Firebase Scheduler / AWS EventBridge
• Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM) for push notifications
• Role-based notification permissions
• Notification history logs for audit
